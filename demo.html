Good to know

  • All people, records and figures are synthetic.
  • The demo group is not registered with CQC or Ofsted, so registration policies are not included.
  • Pay and tax figures are demo estimates, not payroll calculations.
  • To try Data Import & Migration, use the synthetic sample files: new starters, training records, care plans and policies. The spreadsheets include a few rows that need fixing, so you can see the review step.
  • The demo guide lists every demo account and what data each one holds.
