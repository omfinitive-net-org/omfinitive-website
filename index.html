The CQC Registration Workspace

What the workspace covers

One place to prepare, track and review your own CQC registration information before you apply.

  • Provider & legal entityCompany or organisation details, ownership and contact information for the registering provider.
  • Nominated individualThe person who supervises the management of regulated activities on behalf of an organisation, with their checks and documents.
  • Registered managerThe proposed registered manager’s details, experience, qualifications and supporting evidence.
  • Regulated activitiesRecord the regulated activities you intend to carry out and the service types they relate to.
  • LocationsWhere the service will be managed and delivered from, with the information you hold for each location.
  • Statement of purposeUpload the current version, track draft and review status and keep version history.
  • Fit and proper persons & DBSTrack DBS checks, references, right to work and other fit and proper person evidence for the people involved.
  • PoliciesThe policies your service needs, tracked from Required to Approved and Review Due.
  • Staffing & trainingConnected to Recruitment and Compliance: planned and recruited staff, mandatory training and induction.
  • Financial & business evidenceBusiness plan, financial information and insurance evidence kept alongside the application.
  • Readiness reviewA final internal review that highlights any area needing action before you consider your preparation complete.

The Registration Workspace

What the workspace covers

A structured registration journey for children’s homes in England, with its own children’s-home content and evidence rather than a shared CQC checklist.

  • Structured registration journeyEach area moves from Not Started to In Progress, Ready for Review and Complete, so nothing relies on scattered folders and spreadsheets.
  • Organisation & providerProvider and company information, the proposed children’s home and its registration status.
  • Statement of PurposeUpload the current version, track draft and review status, record approval and keep version history.
  • Children’s GuideKeep the home’s Children’s Guide, its status and current approved version visible.
  • Children’s-home policiesA dedicated policy area, including safeguarding, missing child, behaviour management (including restraint) and equalities, tracked from Required to Approved and Review Due.
  • Premises & locationLocation assessment, planning evidence, property information, access to health, education, transport and community resources, and local risks.
  • Key peopleReadiness profiles for the Responsible Individual, Registered Manager and directors or associated individuals: checks, documents, actions and status.
  • Staffing & workforceConnected to Recruitment and Compliance: staff required and recruited, DBS, references, right to work, qualifications, training and induction.
  • Business & financialBusiness plan, cash-flow forecast, insurance evidence and other financial documents kept alongside the application.
  • Application readiness reviewA final internal review that highlights any area needing action before you consider your application pack ready.

What’s the difference?

SC1 relates to registering the children’s social care service, while SC2 relates to relevant individuals associated with the service, depending on their role and the applicable registration requirements. Ofsted’s SC1 guidance explains that people in governance or management roles, such as managers, responsible individuals or directors, may need to complete SC2.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is OMFiNiTiVE?

OMFiNiTiVE is a care-sector management platform centred around three key areas: Recruitment, Compliance and Call Monitoring. It is designed to help care and support organisations bring important workforce, compliance and service-delivery information together.

Who is OMFiNiTiVE designed for?

OMFiNiTiVE is designed around seven key care and support sectors:

  • Temporary Staffing
  • Supported Living (Adults)
  • Supported Living (Children)
  • Children’s Homes
  • Domiciliary Care
  • Care Homes
  • Supported Accommodation for Young People aged 16+
Can OMFiNiTiVE support more than one service type?

Yes. Your organisation selects the services it provides, so the platform experience can be configured around those selected service areas.

What does the Recruitment area cover?

The Recruitment area can bring together candidate applications, identity documentation, Right to Work information, proof of address, references, employment history, DBS information, qualifications, training records and onboarding information.

What does the Compliance area cover?

The Compliance area brings together important compliance information such as staff records, training and expiry information, policies, audits, risk assessments, safeguarding, incidents, complaints, evidence and outstanding compliance actions.

What is Call Monitoring?

Call Monitoring provides oversight of planned care and support visits. It can be used to present information such as scheduled visits, staff attendance, check-in and check-out information, planned versus actual visit times, missed or late calls, visit records, completed tasks and care or support notes.

Is OMFiNiTiVE only for domiciliary care?

No. OMFiNiTiVE is designed for seven different care and support sectors, including services that do not operate traditional home-care calls.

Can different organisations have different setups?

Yes. Organisations select their relevant service sector or sectors, so they see a platform experience appropriate to their organisation rather than an unnecessarily cluttered system.

Is OMFiNiTiVE suitable for organisations with multiple services?

Yes. OMFiNiTiVE is intended to support organisations operating across more than one care or support setting by bringing the core areas of Recruitment, Compliance and Call Monitoring into one environment.

Can we bring our existing data into OMFiNiTiVE?

Yes. Data Import & Migration lets you bring existing staff, new starters, training records, care plans and policy documents into OMFiNiTiVE from spreadsheets and files. Each import shows you what will be added, and anything that needs fixing, before it is saved. As part of Premium onboarding we can also help with the initial migration of agreed existing records. More about data migration.

Does OMFiNiTiVE submit my Ofsted application or guarantee registration?

No. OMFiNiTiVE helps children’s homes in England organise their registration preparation, documents, people and evidence in one workspace. Applications are made to Ofsted through its own process and registration decisions are made by Ofsted. OMFiNiTiVE is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ofsted or the CQC, and readiness figures shown in the platform are an internal guide only.

Does OMFiNiTiVE submit my CQC application or guarantee registration?

No. OMFiNiTiVE helps adult social care providers in England, such as domiciliary care, supported living (adults) and care homes, organise their registration preparation, people, policies and evidence in one workspace. Applications are made to the CQC through its own process and registration decisions are made by the CQC. OMFiNiTiVE is not affiliated with or endorsed by the CQC, and readiness figures shown in the platform are an internal guide only.

How do I find out if OMFiNiTiVE is right for my organisation?

The best starting point is to request a demonstration. This allows you to explore how OMFiNiTiVE could be configured around your organisation, service type and operational requirements.
